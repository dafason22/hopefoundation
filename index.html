Who We Are

Hope for Children Foundation is a non-governmental organization dedicated to lifting children out of hardship. We believe every child — no matter where they are born — deserves to learn, to be healthy, and to dream.

  • Free quality education for every child
  • Daily nutritious meals
  • Healthcare and counselling services
  • Sports, arts and talent programs
  • Safe homes and nurturing communities

Thanks to our donors, thousands of children have already regained their childhood. You can be part of their story too.

Our Programs

Each program is a step towards a brighter future for the children we serve. Together, we turn compassion into action.

Education

Education for All

We build classrooms, supply learning materials and sponsor children who would otherwise miss out on school.

Nutrition

Healthy Meals

A full meal every day keeps children strong, focused and ready to learn. No child should learn on an empty stomach.

Health

Health & Care

Medical check-ups, vaccinations and emotional support — because a healthy child is a happy child.

Talent

Sports & Arts

Football, music, dance and painting give children the space to play, express themselves and discover their gifts.
